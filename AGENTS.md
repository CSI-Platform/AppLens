- AppLens (display name Installed) prepares PC inventory reports for workflow consultations.
- Follow the user's requested scope. Keep docs factual and brief; do not add roadmaps, status logs, handoffs, or implied follow-up work.
- Use one agent. Keep replies concise.

- Scans are read-only. Removal requires explicit confirmation, supported Windows/vendor mechanisms, and recorded outcomes.
- Keep reports redacted by default, review before sharing, and never infer delivery from opening Windows sharing.
- Preserve current-user protected history and legacy records. No telemetry, accounts, or automatic uploads.
- Keep `future/AppLens-Tune/` independent of the core build and startup.
